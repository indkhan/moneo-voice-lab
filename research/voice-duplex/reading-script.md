# Reading script for voice tests

Sentences and short conversations with known words, so a recording of them can be scored exactly. Anyone can read it: every extra voice (accent, speed, microphone) makes the test set better. It takes about 15 minutes in all.

How to record:

- One recording per part, on a phone or laptop, in a normal room. Name the files `<your name>-part1.m4a`, `-part2`, and so on.
- Do not say the numbers or the labels. Just leave the pauses described for each part.
- Speak the way you normally would, not slowly or carefully. If you stumble, carry on; do not repeat.
- Say the names as written (Rewe, Lidl, Netto, and so on), even if you shop somewhere else: the test knows these names.
- Send the files to the owner privately. Recordings are personal data and are never committed to the repository.

## Part 1: single requests (about 3 minutes)

Leave about two seconds of silence between sentences.

### Questions

1. How much did I spend this month?
2. How much did I spend on groceries last month?
3. What did I pay at Rewe in October?
4. How much did I earn from my salary this year?
5. Can you check how much I spent at Lidl this week?
6. How many times did I pay Netflix this year?
7. Did I spend more on restaurants this month than last month?
8. Where did my money go last month?
9. How much did I spend on Amazon in the last thirty days?
10. How much went on transport yesterday?

### Adding things

11. I spent five euros at Rewe for groceries, can you add that?
12. I just paid twelve euros fifty at Lidl, please add it.
13. Add a hundred euros for Amazon, I bought headphones.
14. I got my salary today, one thousand three hundred forty-three euros, add that.
15. I paid twenty-eight euros for a train ticket to Munich.
16. I spent one euro fifty on laundry just now.
17. Netto, seventeen euros, groceries, add it.
18. I pay twenty-four euros a month for ChatGPT, add that as a subscription.
19. I have seventeen euros in my wallet right now.
20. Yesterday I spent nine euros at Starbucks for coffee.

### Changing your mind

21. How much did I spend last month, no wait, this month?
22. I spent ten euros at Rewe, sorry, not Rewe, it was Lidl.
23. Add twenty euros for Spotify, no, it's not twenty, it's twenty-four.
24. What did I spend on groceries, I mean restaurants, in September?
25. I paid fifteen euros at Netto, actually make that fifty.

### Things only the chat assistant can answer

26. What is my net worth right now?
27. How much is in my N26 account?
28. Am I over budget this month?
29. If I cancel Netflix, how much do I save in a year?
30. Can you check Scalable and tell me how my stocks are doing?

## Part 2: conversations (about 5 minutes)

Each conversation is a few turns in a row, as if you were on a call with Moneo. After each turn, wait about **four seconds** as if Moneo were answering, then say the next turn. Between conversations, wait about **eight seconds**.

### Following up on the last answer

- **C1.** How much did I spend on groceries this month? · And last month? · What about restaurants?
- **C2.** How much did I spend at Rewe in October? · And at Lidl? · OK, and both together?
- **C3.** How much did I earn this year? · Only from my salary. · And last year?
- **C4.** How many times did I pay Uber last month? · And how much was that in total?

### Changing the request in the next turn

- **C5.** How much did I spend on transport last month? · No, sorry, I meant this month.
- **C6.** What did I pay at Amazon this year? · Actually, make that the last three months.
- **C7.** How much did I spend on coffee this week? · Not coffee, restaurants.
- **C8.** Where did my money go in September? · Hmm, show me October instead.

### Asking a second, different thing

- **C9.** How much did I spend this month? · OK. And how much did I earn this month?
- **C10.** How much did I spend at Starbucks last month? · Thanks. Can you also tell me how much I spent on groceries?
- **C11.** What did I pay at Netto yesterday? · Right. Now add five euros at Rewe for groceries.

### Adding, then correcting in the next turn

- **C12.** I spent eight euros at Lidl, add that. · Sorry, it was eighteen, not eight.
- **C13.** Add thirty euros at Rewe for groceries. · No wait, it was Netto.
- **C14.** I paid twelve euros for restaurants yesterday, add it. · Actually it was today, not yesterday.
- **C15.** Add forty-five euros at Amazon. · Hmm, not forty-five, fifty-four. · And put it under shopping.

### Adding several things one after another

- **C16.** I spent four euros fifty at Starbucks, add that. · And six euros at Rewe for groceries. · And that's it, thanks.
- **C17.** Add twenty pounds at Amazon. · And ten dollars for Spotify.
- **C18.** I got two hundred euros from my salary, add it. · And I spent sixty of it at Lidl.

### Messy, the way people really talk

- **C19.** Hey, um, so, I wanted to ask, how much did I, uh, spend on groceries, like, the whole of this month?
- **C20.** Can you, sorry, can you add, I spent, it was like eleven euros at Rewe, yeah, eleven, for groceries.
- **C21.** OK so last month, how much, no, wait, start again. How much did I spend at Lidl last month?

## Part 3: talking over the assistant (about 3 minutes)

This part needs **a second person** who reads Moneo's lines aloud, at a normal speed, near the phone or laptop that records. You speak your line at the moment marked ✋, **while they are still talking**. The second person keeps reading to the end of their line when you only say "mm-hm" or "yeah", and stops as soon as you say anything else, as a polite listener would.

- **I1.** You: How much did I spend on groceries last month? · Moneo: You spent two hundred nineteen euros ✋ and thirty-four cents on groceries in October. · You (✋): No, this month.
- **I2.** You: What did I pay at Rewe this year? · Moneo: At Rewe this year ✋ you paid six hundred forty euros across twenty-three visits. · You (✋): Mm-hm.
- **I3.** You: Add ten euros at Lidl for groceries. · Moneo: I've drafted ten euros ✋ at Lidl for groceries, tap the card to save it. · You (✋): Not ten, twelve.
- **I4.** You: How much did I earn this month? · Moneo: You earned one thousand ✋ three hundred forty-three euros this month, all from your salary. · You (✋): Yeah.
- **I5.** You: Where did my money go last month? · Moneo: Most of it went to rent, ✋ then groceries, then restaurants and transport. · You (✋): Wait, stop. How much was rent?
- **I6.** You: How much did I spend at Amazon in the last thirty days? · Moneo: In the last thirty days ✋ you spent ninety-eight euros at Amazon. · You (✋): Sorry, I meant Netflix.
- **I7.** You: Add five euros at Starbucks. · Moneo: I've drafted five euros at ✋ Starbucks for coffee. · You (✋): Uh-huh, and make it today.
- **I8.** You: How many times did I pay Uber this month? · Moneo: You paid Uber ✋ seven times this month. · You (✋): OK, OK. And how much in total?

## Part 4: free talk (about 5 minutes)

Talk to Moneo as if it already had all your money in it. No script: ask, add, correct and change your mind the way you really would. Ideas, if you need them:

- Something you bought this week, and what it cost.
- How much you spent on food or going out lately.
- A subscription you pay and whether it is worth it.
- Money you lent to a friend or a bill you split.
- Cash you have on you, or money someone gave you.
- A question you would actually want answered about your spending.

Afterwards, write one line for each thing you asked or added (for example "asked: food spending last month", "added: 4.50 coffee, corrected to 5"). That line is what the test checks the recording against.
